# The Stock Market Crash Of 1929

## Problems Of Buying Stocks On Margin

When buying on margin it means when an investor purchases an asset by borrowing the balance from a bank or broker. There were people putting down as little as 10 percent and ended up borrowing the rest from brokers making huge bets with little of their money at risk. Interest is charged on borrowed funds which accrues daily and can significantly erode profits you make over time. This can especially happen when positions are held for a long duration of time or when positions are in a rising interest rate environment.

## Black tuesday and Black thursday

Black Thursday happened October 34 1929 it was the first week of historic wall street stock crash. A record number of shares were traded as panic selling began. 12.9 million shares were traded causing the Dow Jones industrial average to drop drastically. Black Tuesday happened on October 28 1829 the stock market was experiencing a catastrophic collapse as panic selling ensured. Billions of dollars in stock value were lost as the Dow Jones industrial average plummeted. A record of 16.4 million shares were traded that day.
